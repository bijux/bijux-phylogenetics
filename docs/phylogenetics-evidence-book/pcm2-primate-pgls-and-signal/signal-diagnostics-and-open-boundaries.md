# PCM2 Signal Diagnostics And Open Boundaries

PCM2 is important not only because it documents agreement, but also because it
keeps incomplete edges visible.

## What This Part Covers

- phylogenetic signal testing
- residual and diagnostic review surfaces
- ancestral reconstruction comparisons
- the remaining intercept-mode likelihood sweep boundary

## Questions This Part Answers

- Which signal and diagnostic surfaces remain strong enough to discuss?
- Which comparative edges are still explicitly bounded or open?
- How does the study separate strong comparative evidence from still-incomplete
  intercept or sweep boundaries?

## Reading Rule

Treat PCM2 as a deeper bounded trust study, not as a blanket claim that comparative model criticism in the repository is already complete. Its open boundaries are part of the study's value, not an embarrassment to hide.
